常見問題

點樣取消訂閱？

打開 iPhone「設定」→ 撳你嘅名（Apple ID）→「訂閱項目」→ 搵到「What's for dinner」→ 撳「取消訂閱」。取消後，訂閱會喺當前計費期結束後停止。

點樣恢復已購買嘅訂閱？

打開 App → 設定 → 撳「恢復 App Store 購買」。App 會自動檢查你嘅 Apple ID 並恢復有效嘅訂閱。

AI 掃描功能唔 work？

請確保你嘅裝置已連接網絡。AI 掃描需要網絡連接先可以分析相片。如果問題持續，請透過下方 email 聯絡我們。

我嘅資料會唔會被上傳？

你嘅食材、食譜等資料只儲存喺你嘅裝置上，唔會上傳到我們嘅伺服器。AI 掃描嘅相片喺分析完成後即時刪除。詳情請參閱我們嘅私隱政策。

點樣聯絡支援團隊？

請透過下方嘅 email 地址聯絡我們，我們會盡快回覆。

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → tap your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → find "What's for dinner" → tap Cancel Subscription. After cancellation, the subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open the App → Settings → tap "Restore App Store Purchases". The app will automatically check your Apple ID and restore any active subscriptions.

The AI scan feature isn't working?

Please make sure your device is connected to the internet. AI scanning requires a network connection to analyse photos. If the issue persists, please contact us via the email below.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

Your ingredients, recipes, and settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. Photos used for AI scanning are deleted immediately after analysis. See our Privacy Policy for details.
