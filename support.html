常見問題

點樣取消訂閱？

打開 iPhone「設定」→ 撳你嘅名（Apple ID）→「訂閱項目」→ 搵到「What's for dinner」→ 撳「取消訂閱」。取消後，訂閱會喺當前計費期結束後停止。

點樣恢復已購買嘅訂閱？

打開 App → 家庭設定 → 帳戶 → 查看方案 →「恢復購買」。請使用購買時的 Apple 帳戶，App 會核對有效訂閱。

七日餐單未能生成？

請確認已登入、網絡正常，並有有效家庭訂閱及剩餘 AI 用量。如果仍未能完成，請透過下方電郵聯絡我們。現有版本不提供 AI 相片掃描。

我嘅資料會唔會被上傳？

未登入時，部分資料保存在裝置上。家庭功能會透過 Supabase 處理帳戶、餐單及飲食需要；AI 每週規劃會把所需飲食偏好、限制及候選食譜交由 DashScope 處理。詳情請參閱私隱政策。

點樣刪除帳戶？

登入後打開 App → 家庭設定 → 帳戶 →「刪除帳戶」，刪除 App 帳戶及相關個人資料；其他成員仍在使用的家庭共享餐單會保留。這不會刪除你的 Apple／Google 帳戶，亦不會取消訂閱，請另到商店訂閱設定取消。

點樣聯絡支援團隊？

請透過下方嘅 email 地址聯絡我們，我們會盡快回覆。

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → tap your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → find "What's for dinner" → tap Cancel Subscription. After cancellation, the subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open App → Family Settings → Account → View plans → Restore Purchases. Use the Apple account used for the purchase. The app will check your active subscription.

Why hasn't my weekly menu been generated?

Check that you are signed in, connected to the internet, and have an active household subscription with remaining AI usage. Contact us below if the request still cannot finish. The current version does not offer AI photo scanning.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

Some signed-out data stays on your device. Household features process account, menu and dietary information through Supabase. AI weekly planning sends the necessary dietary preferences, restrictions and candidate recipes to DashScope. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

After signing in, open App → Family Settings → Account → Delete account to delete the app account and related personal data. Shared menus needed by remaining household members remain available. This does not delete your Apple/Google account or cancel a subscription; cancel separately in the store's subscription settings.
