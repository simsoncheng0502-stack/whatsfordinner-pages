What's for dinner（今晚食乜）

私隱政策 · 最後更新：2026 年 6 月 30 日

1. 概述

What's for dinner（下稱「本 App」）由關智成（下稱「我們」）開發及營運。我們重視你嘅私隱，本政策說明我們如何收集、使用及保護你嘅資料。

2. 我們收集嘅資料

2.1 你主動提供嘅資料

  • 相機及相片：當你使用 AI 掃描功能拍攝食物或食材包裝時，相片會傳送至我們嘅伺服器進行分析。相片唔會被儲存，分析完成後即時刪除。
  • 食材及食譜資料：你輸入嘅雪櫃食材、食譜等資料儲存喺你裝置嘅本機儲存空間（localStorage），唔會上傳至伺服器。

2.2 自動收集嘅資料

  • 廣告識別碼：免費版用戶會睇到由 Google AdMob 提供嘅廣告。AdMob 可能收集裝置廣告識別碼以提供廣告。你可以喺 iOS 設定中限制廣告追蹤。
  • 訂閱資料：當你購買訂閱時，交易由 Apple 同 RevenueCat 處理。我們只接收訂閱狀態（有效/過期），唔會接觸你嘅付款資料。
  • 匿名使用統計：我們可能收集匿名嘅錯誤報告同使用統計，用於改善 App 體驗。呢啲資料唔會同個人身份關聯。

3. 第三方服務

本 App 使用以下第三方服務，各自有其私隱政策：

  • Google AdMob（廣告）— Google 私隱政策
  • RevenueCat（訂閱管理）— RevenueCat 私隱政策
  • Supabase（後端服務）— Supabase 私隱政策
  • 阿里雲 DashScope（AI 分析）— 阿里雲私隱政策

4. 訂閱條款

  • 本 App 提供自動續期訂閱（Premium 月費 HK$28、Pro 月費 HK$58）。
  • 付款會透過你嘅 Apple ID 帳戶收取。
  • 訂閱會自動續期，除非你喺當前期間結束前至少 24 小時關閉自動續期。
  • 你可以喺 iPhone 設定 → Apple ID → 訂閱項目 管理或取消訂閱。

5. 資料儲存及安全

你嘅食材同食譜資料只儲存喺你裝置上，我們無法存取。AI 分析用嘅相片喺處理後即時刪除，唔會保留。我們採用行業標準嘅安全措施保護傳輸中嘅資料。

6. 兒童私隱

本 App 唔會刻意向 13 歲以下兒童收集資料。如果你發現有兒童向我們提供咗個人資料，請聯絡我們，我們會立即刪除。

7. 政策變更

我們可能會不時更新本私隱政策。更新後嘅版本會喺本頁面發佈，並更新「最後更新」日期。

8. 聯絡我們

如有任何私隱相關問題，請聯絡：
BeesKneestxz@californiamail.com

What's for dinner

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 30 June 2026

1. Overview

What's for dinner (the "App") is developed and operated by Kwanchi Cheng ("we", "us"). We value your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Camera & Photos: When you use the AI scan feature to photograph food or packaging, the photo is sent to our server for analysis. Photos are not stored and are deleted immediately after processing.
  • Ingredient & Recipe Data: Your fridge items, recipes, and settings are stored locally on your device (localStorage) and are never uploaded to our servers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Advertising Identifiers: Free-tier users see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers to deliver ads. You can limit ad tracking in iOS Settings.
  • Subscription Data: When you subscribe, transactions are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We only receive subscription status (active/expired) and never access your payment details.
  • Anonymous Analytics: We may collect anonymous crash reports and usage statistics to improve the App. This data is not linked to your identity.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Supabase (backend services) — Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Alibaba Cloud DashScope (AI analysis) — Alibaba Cloud Privacy Policy

4. Subscription Terms

  • The App offers auto-renewable subscriptions (Premium at HK$28/month, Pro at HK$58/month).
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your ingredient and recipe data is stored only on your device and we cannot access it. Photos used for AI analysis are deleted immediately after processing and are not retained. We use industry-standard security measures to protect data in transit.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
